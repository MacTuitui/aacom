[Tomonami](https://www.sonycsl.co.jp/en/projects/tomonami/) is a generative system developed by Alexis André at Sony Computer Science Laboratories to support artists in exploring new ideas. For Kutani porcelain artist Yukio Yoshita, fourth-generation head of Kinzangama, André created a custom tool shaped around Yoshita's visual language.

The collaboration begins with conversation and a close reading of the artist's previous work. André translates characteristic features of that work into generative rules and adjustable parameters. Yoshita can then move through a range of visual proposals, using his own judgment to decide which directions to pursue.

The images are starting points for further making. Tomonami lets Yoshita explore ideas without having to resolve every production constraint in advance; he brings his own experience of porcelain to interpreting the results. The relationship between the generated image and the finished object becomes part of the creative process.

Yoshita contributed to Tomonami from its conceptual stage. After two years of shared experimentation, the first works made through the collaboration were [shown at MUTAN, the gallery at Kinzangama in Komatsu, Ishikawa, in November 2023](https://www.sonycsl.co.jp/en/press/prs20231124/).
