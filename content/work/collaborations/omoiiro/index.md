Omoiiro™ is a color palette generation algorithm that has been applied to create collections of items for the fashion label ISSEY MIYAKE.

At its core, Omoiiro™ takes one picture and extracts relevant colors. Furthermore, the algorithm can be tuned to a specific usage. In this example, Omoiiro™ orders the colors to suit the main parts of the items created.

{{<art_image

ISSEY MIYAKE collaborated with André to produce the "Colorscope Omoiiro" collection, where each palette was extracted from a picture taken by the lead designer, Miyamae Yoshiyuki, during a trip in eastern Europa.


ISSEY MIYAKE and André collaborated on another collection "Record", with differents pictures of four major cities, London, Paris, New York and Tokyo to create the different palettes.
