A commission piece from GUCCI to be shown at the Gucci Cosmos space in London, later sold at Christie's.

What Gucci archival themes (origin myths; signature codes; and the like) informed your work?
>This piece is my interpretation of the myth around the origin of the Gucci legacy. Time spent in an elevator that started everything. A slow process that eventually created a whole universe of style and fashion. I took the red of the elevator, the iconic red and green color combination and added some gold accents to reflect my interpretation of the signature codes of Gucci.

Could you briefly describe the creation process?
>I started by working around the idea of growth, centered around an obvious center (the "big-bang" of the brand): the elevator. I looked at how venation patterns happen in leaves and I imagined how the artistic direction of Guccio Gucci acted as a foundation for everything that was created along the years: a structure that would support the entire Gucci heritage that is then represented as feathers or fireworks around the tree-like growth pattern. The animated part of the piece represents a pulse - how a simple idea that might have happened in an elevator is the root of everything we might see today: it started with a thought, it ended up as an icon. On the technical side, this piece is created using a very huge amount of circles that follow clear rules of growth ; the same rules dictate which color the circles should be. The final structure then glows while the pulse is going through all those circles in order: the wave that goes up, like an elevator would do.

What does this final artwork represent?
>This piece is reflecting on how a single idea can develop into a deep, complex structure that is connected to everything. How this specific moment feels. How it feels obvious a posteriori that this was "it" - but how did it feel at the start? The *birth* of any revolution.
