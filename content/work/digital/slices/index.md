As far as we know, time only moves forward. Yet we experience a lot of cycles, where we end up somehow back at the starting position again. Birth, growth, reproduction, decay, death. Repeat. Slices is one of those cycles, a generative process of growth with snapshots taken along the way, slices of time, moments to remember.

Slices is presented in three formats, all unique and procedurally generated by a master algorithm. The first option is unique slices of an instance of the growth process. Then there are complete cycles of the evolution and finally physical scrolls of the whole experience.

{{<art_image
    file="artist.jpg"
    alt="artist"
/> }}


>"Slices" is a collection of moments - where absolute growth is stuck in a mysterious time, warping space.
>All those moments happened at the same time - in parallel dimensions.
>
>"Slices" is also a re-collection of those moments - where absolute growth is laid out in space, warping time.
>All those moments happened in the same space - at the same time.
>
>"Slices" is also a *physical* object - putting time and space in a scroll. Time literally unrolls itself.
>You'll have to come to the show to see the parallel stories shown on the scrolls - those stories are only legends and never happened.
>The only truth is the one on chain.
>
>"Slices" is all of the above, in either format. You choose if you want a Slice - each new slice available each day over seven days- or if you want the whole Cycle - available every eighth day, repeating four times for the duration of the show.
>You can opt for the scroll as well.
>
>"Slices" is of course a generative piece - if you know my work, you should be fine.
