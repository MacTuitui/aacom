Navette explores what happens when you take a Jacquard machine and fuse it with generative algorithms that can work at the point level. The Jacquard and its limited palette (there is a physical limit to the number of threads you can load in the machine) becomes a constraint that the algorithm disrupts by playing around dithering techniques.

{{<art_image
    file="navette-code.jpg"
    alt="Computer image and thread"
    caption="A mix between physical and digital"
/> }}

Each tapestry is the result of a generative algorithm that outputs the necessary information to feed to the loom, allowing to create a series of tapestries without any creative choice left. Feed the corresponding colors in the machine, and send the instructions.

{{<art_image
    file="navette-test.jpg"
    alt="Raw output of the loom"
    caption="Test patterns on the loom"
/> }}

The tapestries measure 170 cm x 250 cm, and are made of seven to ten different colors (including silver and gold Lumex threads).
